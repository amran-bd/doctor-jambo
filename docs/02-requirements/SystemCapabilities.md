# System Capabilities

## Purpose

Describe the coherent capabilities the platform must provide.

## Scope

Identity, care access, AI assistance, voice interaction, workflow orchestration, administration, and observability.

## Actors

Patients, doctors, administrators, operators, and integrated services.

## Requirements

- Identity and consent management.
- Provider discovery, verification, appointments, and notifications.
- Secure patient-doctor consultation support.
- Grounded AI consultation and doctor copilot assistance.
- Voice input, language support, transcription, and spoken responses.
- Governed multi-agent health-journey orchestration.
- Audit, policy, and operational management.

## Assumptions

Capabilities can be released independently behind approved policies.

## Constraints

Clinical and AI capabilities require governance controls.

## Future considerations

Add interoperability and population-health capabilities after validation.
