# Architecture Principles

- **DDD and bounded contexts:** business language and data ownership remain local to each context.
- **Clean and hexagonal architecture:** domain logic does not depend on frameworks, storage, or vendor SDKs.
- **API first:** contracts, errors, compatibility, and ownership precede implementation.
- **Event driven:** durable business events decouple workflows; consumers are idempotent.
- **Cloud native:** immutable deployments, horizontal scaling, health checks, and declarative operations.
- **Secure by design:** least privilege, encryption, consent, audit, and privacy minimisation are defaults.
- **AI governance:** grounded, observable, policy-bound AI with human approval where required.
