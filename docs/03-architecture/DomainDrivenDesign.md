# Domain-Driven Design

DDD aligns services with business capabilities, uses a ubiquitous language, and prevents healthcare, AI, and operational concerns from becoming one shared model. Aggregates enforce local invariants; domain events communicate completed facts; anti-corruption layers protect domain models from partner and model-provider contracts.

Key aggregates include Patient, Doctor, Appointment, Consultation, MedicalRecord, KnowledgeDocument, AgentWorkflow, and Notification.
