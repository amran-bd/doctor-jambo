# Problem Statement

Patients often struggle to identify trusted providers, understand available services, book care without friction, and retain a clear record of next steps. Providers may rely on disconnected channels for scheduling and patient communication, increasing administrative effort and reducing continuity.

The problem is especially acute when a patient needs timely, non-emergency guidance but does not know where to start. Fragmented information, limited visibility into provider availability, and weak follow-up mechanisms can cause delayed or missed care.

Doctor Jambo will address these gaps with a secure, verified, and accessible digital care journey. It must not present itself as a substitute for emergency medical services or professional diagnosis where an in-person assessment is required.
