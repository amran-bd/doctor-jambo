# Business Objectives

| Objective | Intended outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Improve access | Patients can find verified, relevant care options and request appointments with minimal friction. |
| Build trust | Provider identity, credentials, service information, and platform policies are clear and reviewable. |
| Support continuity | Patients and providers can retain appropriate consultation and follow-up information. |
| Reduce administration | Scheduling, notifications, and basic patient intake reduce manual coordination. |
| Operate responsibly | Privacy, consent, security, and clinical escalation are built into the service model. |

The initial business target is a reliable, measurable care-access experience rather than broad medical automation.
