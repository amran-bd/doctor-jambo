# User Journey

## Core patient journey

1. A patient arrives through a referral, search, or direct link.
2. The platform presents its purpose, privacy notice, and emergency-care guidance.
3. The patient registers or signs in and provides only the information needed to proceed.
4. The patient searches for a provider or service, reviews verified profile details, and selects an option.
5. The patient chooses an available time and submits an appointment request or booking.
6. The patient receives confirmation, preparation guidance, and reminders.
7. A consultation or supported care interaction takes place.
8. The patient receives agreed follow-up actions and can manage future appointments.

## Key experience principles

- Use understandable language and transparent next steps.
- Surface trust, safety, and privacy information at the moment it matters.
- Minimise repeated data entry.
- Provide alternatives when a provider or time is unavailable.
- Escalate urgent-care guidance rather than attempting to resolve emergencies in-product.
