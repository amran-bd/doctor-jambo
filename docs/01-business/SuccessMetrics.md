# Success Metrics

| Area | Metric | Initial measurement approach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Access | Search-to-booking conversion rate | Completed bookings divided by qualified provider searches. |
| Timeliness | Median time from booking request to confirmation | Measure across supported appointment types. |
| Trust | Percentage of active providers with completed verification | Verified active providers divided by active providers. |
| Experience | Patient satisfaction after completed care interaction | Optional post-interaction survey. |
| Reliability | Appointment notification delivery success | Successfully delivered reminders divided by scheduled reminders. |
| Operations | Provider administrative time per appointment | Baseline and compare through workflow research. |
| Safety | Urgent-care guidance exposure and completion | Monitor without treating it as clinical outcome data. |
| Privacy | Unauthorised-access incidents | Target: zero confirmed incidents. |

Metric targets, instrumentation, and review cadence must be agreed before launch.
