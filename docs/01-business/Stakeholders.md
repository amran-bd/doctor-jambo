# Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Needs and responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Patients and caregivers | Clear access to trustworthy care, understandable information, privacy, and support. |
| Healthcare providers | Accurate patient context, schedule control, secure communication, and efficient administration. |
| Clinic administrators | Provider onboarding, service configuration, operational visibility, and support tooling. |
| Clinical governance | Safe care pathways, escalation guidance, credential standards, and quality oversight. |
| Product and engineering teams | Prioritised requirements, measurable outcomes, and clear operating constraints. |
| Security, privacy, and legal teams | Appropriate data handling, consent, retention, and regulatory compliance. |
| Partners and regulators | Transparent service boundaries and evidence of responsible operation. |
