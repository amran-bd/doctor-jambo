# Business Rules

- **BRL-01:** Only approved provider accounts may be displayed as verified or accept appointments.
- **BRL-02:** Patients must be shown a clear statement that the platform is not for emergencies and how to seek urgent help.
- **BRL-03:** Health information is collected only for a stated care or operational purpose with the required consent.
- **BRL-04:** Access to patient information is limited to authorised roles and the minimum necessary context.
- **BRL-05:** Appointment changes must be recorded and communicated to affected participants.
- **BRL-06:** Providers remain responsible for clinical decisions and for following applicable professional standards.
- **BRL-07:** Platform content must not claim a diagnosis, guarantee an outcome, or encourage users to delay emergency care.
- **BRL-08:** Data retention, deletion, and export requests must follow approved privacy, legal, and clinical-record policies.
- **BRL-09:** Audit records must be retained for sensitive account, consent, and access events according to policy.
