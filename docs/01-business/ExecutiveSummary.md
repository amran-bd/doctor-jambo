# Executive Summary

Doctor Jambo is a digital health platform designed to make trusted healthcare guidance and access easier for people who need timely, understandable support. The platform will connect patients with verified healthcare professionals and services through a secure, user-friendly experience.

The first release focuses on discovery, appointment requests, consultations, patient records relevant to care delivery, and follow-up communication. It is intended to reduce avoidable delays, improve access to qualified care, and give providers a practical way to manage digital patient interactions.

This foundation document set aligns business stakeholders on what Doctor Jambo should achieve, who it serves, and the constraints that shape the product.
