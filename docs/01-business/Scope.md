# Scope

## In scope for the initial product

- Patient registration, sign-in, and profile management.
- Provider profiles, credential verification workflow, specialties, and service details.
- Search and discovery of providers and services.
- Appointment availability, booking requests, confirmations, reminders, and cancellations.
- Secure consultation workflow and follow-up communication where supported.
- Consent capture and audit-friendly records related to platform use.
- Provider scheduling and patient-interaction management.
- Basic reporting on operational and service metrics.

## Out of scope for the initial product

- Emergency dispatch or replacement of emergency services.
- Autonomous diagnosis, prescribing, or treatment decisions.
- Full hospital information-system replacement.
- Medical-device integration unless separately approved.
- Insurance adjudication and claims settlement.
- Cross-border clinical practice beyond approved legal and licensing arrangements.
