# Risk Analysis

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Misunderstanding of service limits | Users may delay urgent care. | Persistent emergency guidance, clear service boundaries, and escalation content. |
| Provider credential errors | Trust, legal, and patient-safety harm. | Documented verification workflow, renewal reviews, and approval audit trail. |
| Privacy or security incident | Harm to patients and regulatory exposure. | Privacy-by-design, least privilege, encryption, monitoring, and incident response. |
| Low provider adoption | Limited patient choice and weak service value. | Lightweight onboarding, useful scheduling tools, and provider feedback loops. |
| Poor information quality | Incorrect expectations or unsuitable bookings. | Content ownership, review processes, and clear update responsibilities. |
| Regulatory change | Delays or rework. | Legal review, policy register, and market-by-market launch controls. |
| Algorithmic bias or unsafe AI use | Unequal access or inappropriate guidance. | Human oversight, evaluation, clear limitations, and restricted use cases. |
