# User Personas

## Amina — Patient seeking timely care

Amina is a working adult who needs a straightforward way to find an appropriate clinician, understand available options, and book an appointment without repeated calls. She values trust signals, plain language, predictable next steps, and control over her information.

## Karim — Caregiver coordinating family care

Karim helps an older relative manage appointments. He needs clear reminders, permission-aware access where applicable, and the ability to understand what action is needed next.

## Dr. Njeri — Healthcare provider

Dr. Njeri wants a verified professional presence, control over availability, concise patient context, and secure communication. She needs the platform to reduce administrative work without compromising clinical judgement.

## Sam — Clinic administrator

Sam manages provider onboarding and service operations. He needs accurate configuration, visibility into appointment activity, and workflows that support compliance and service quality.
