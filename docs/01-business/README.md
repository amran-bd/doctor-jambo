# Doctor Jambo — Business Requirements Foundation

This directory is the business baseline for Doctor Jambo. It defines the problem, intended outcomes, scope, stakeholders, rules, and measures of success before solution design begins.

## Reading order

1. ExecutiveSummary, Vision, Mission, and ProblemStatement
2. BusinessObjectives, Scope, and Stakeholders
3. BusinessRequirements and BusinessRules
4. UserPersonas and UserJourney
5. SuccessMetrics, RiskAnalysis, Assumptions, and Glossary

## Status

Foundation draft for stakeholder review. Requirements marked as priorities must be refined into product and technical requirements before delivery.
