# Business Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BR-01 | The platform shall allow patients to create and manage an account using supported identity and contact methods. | Must |
| BR-02 | The platform shall show provider profiles with verified status, specialty, services, location or consultation mode, and availability information. | Must |
| BR-03 | The platform shall allow patients to search and filter providers by relevant service attributes. | Must |
| BR-04 | The platform shall allow patients to request, confirm, reschedule, or cancel appointments subject to provider rules. | Must |
| BR-05 | The platform shall notify participants of material appointment and follow-up changes. | Must |
| BR-06 | The platform shall capture explicit consent before collecting or sharing health information for a care interaction. | Must |
| BR-07 | The platform shall provide providers with an appropriate view of patient-provided information and care history relevant to their interaction. | Must |
| BR-08 | The platform shall provide clear emergency and escalation guidance when users present urgent concerns. | Must |
| BR-09 | The platform shall support role-based administrative management of providers, services, and operational settings. | Should |
| BR-10 | The platform shall provide aggregate operational metrics without exposing unnecessary personal health information. | Should |
| BR-11 | The platform may support payment collection only after legal, financial, and service policies are approved. | Could |

All requirements require acceptance criteria, ownership, and traceability before implementation.
