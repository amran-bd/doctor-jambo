# Glossary

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Patient | A person seeking or receiving care through a participating service. |
| Caregiver | A person supporting a patient, with access only where authorised. |
| Provider | A healthcare professional or approved care organisation participating in the platform. |
| Verification | The approved process used to validate provider identity, credentials, and eligibility. |
| Consultation | A scheduled care interaction between a patient and provider, whether remote or in person. |
| Consent | A recorded, informed agreement to a stated collection, use, or sharing of information. |
| Health information | Personal information relating to a person's health, care, or treatment. |
| Emergency guidance | Information directing a user to appropriate urgent or emergency services; it is not a diagnosis. |
| BRD | Business Requirements Document; the business-level description of needs and outcomes. |
