# Assumptions

- Doctor Jambo will operate initially in jurisdictions where the required service, privacy, and provider-licensing obligations can be met.
- Participating providers will supply accurate credentials, services, and availability information.
- Patients have access to a supported device and contact channel for account verification and reminders.
- Teleconsultation, where offered, will be limited to approved use cases and provider jurisdictions.
- Clinical governance, legal, privacy, and security stakeholders will review product decisions affecting patient safety or health data.
- Detailed payment, prescribing, laboratory, and insurance workflows will be decided separately from this foundation scope.
